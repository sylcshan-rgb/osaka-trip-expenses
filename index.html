Everyone in the group adds their own expenses directly in the shared spreadsheet — no login needed for viewing, just open and type a new row. This dashboard refreshes automatically every couple of minutes.

Open shared spreadsheet ↗
How to fill in a row: Date, Description, Category, Amount, Currency, Paid By are required. For splitting: fill Split With with comma-separated names to split evenly (e.g. Sylvia, Jamie) — leave blank if it's just for the payer. For an uneven split, fill Custom Split instead (e.g. Sylvia:60, Jamie:40) — it overrides Split With when present.
